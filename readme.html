# Prison 2 Prosper 🌱 ### *Grow Through What You Go Through* **Live Site:** https://10postpics-collab.github.io/Prison-2-Prosper-/ --- ## What Is This? Prison 2 Prosper is a reentry coaching and support platform founded by **How Bates** of **Bates Solutions LLC** in Miamisburg, Ohio. This website serves as the digital home for returning citizens in Ohio who need guidance navigating reentry — from housing and employment to expungement and entrepreneurship. --- ## What the Site Does - **Reentry Coaching** — One-on-one mentorship for returning citizens - **Legal Navigation** — Expungement guidance, First Step Act info, legal referrals - **Entrepreneurship Support** — Business launch coaching for people with records - **Ohio Resource Guide** — Curated housing, employment, legal, mental health & food resources - **Expungement Eligibility Checker** — Free interactive tool to check Ohio record sealing eligibility - **AI Chat Assistant** — 24/7 P2P Assistant powered by Claude AI - **Live Booking** — Calendly integration for free consultations - **Contact Form** — Formspree powered, delivers to founder in real time --- ## Tech Stack | Feature | Tool | |---|---| | Frontend | HTML, CSS, JavaScript (single file) | | Hosting | GitHub Pages | | Booking | Calendly | | Contact Forms | Formspree | | AI Chat | Anthropic Claude API | | Analytics | Google Analytics (G-ZQ6ZY2R5SD) | | Fonts | Google Fonts (Playfair Display, DM Sans) | --- ## How to Deploy 1.
